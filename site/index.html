      Shanggarh, Sainj Valley

      While travelers flock to the nearby Tirthan Valley or Kasol, Shanggarh remains a pocket of profound stillness.

      The village is home to the Shangchul Mahadev Temple, a masterpiece of traditional Kath Kuni wood and stone architecture.

    Travel well

    Arrive as a guest.

    Small places feel every visitor. A few habits keep them worth visiting.

    Stay with families

    Homestays keep your money in the village. Book direct with the host when you can.

    Carry your waste out

    Many high villages have no waste collection. Pack out every wrapper and bottle you bring.

    Ask before photos

    Shrines, monasteries and people's homes are not backdrops. Ask first, and accept a no.

    Carry your permits

    Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Mizoram and Manipur need an Inner Line Permit for Indian visitors. Foreign nationals need a Protected Area Permit for many border areas.
